# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Customers use Open Support to find answers about their products and workflows, then open or follow a support request when automated guidance is not enough. Support agents and administrators manage the manual support side of the system.

## Product Purpose

Open Support is an automated support center with a human support fallback. Success means customers can resolve common issues quickly through reliable knowledgebase guidance and can move into a clear support conversation when needed.

## Positioning

The primary differentiator is automated support-center guidance grounded in the product knowledgebase, with manual ticket support available when self-service does not resolve the issue.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing customer workflows include knowledgebase search, article reading, ticket creation, ticket lists, ticket conversations, and authentication.
- The current codebase does not yet expose a live customer assistant route or automation API.
- The customer portal is built with React, MUI, TanStack Router, and React Query.
- The existing forest-green brand identity should remain recognizable.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Open Support.
- Preserve the forest-green identity.
- The experience should feel calm, trustworthy, clear, and premium.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains working customer routes, knowledgebase data services, ticket data fixtures/services, and MUI theme tokens. It does not contain a production assistant API, assistant responses, or customer-facing chat history.

## Product Principles

- Help customers resolve issues before asking them to create work.
- Make automation understandable and trustworthy.
- Keep human support visible without making it the only path.
- Preserve clarity under uncertainty, errors, and handoff.
